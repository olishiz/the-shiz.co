Case study · Carbnb

A clearer way to explain car subscription.

Industry
Automotive
Services
Website design and development
Live site
carbnb.my

The brief

Carbnb offers car subscription in Malaysia: one monthly fee that covers insurance, road tax, maintenance and roadside assistance, with terms from 12 to 36 months. The site had to make that offer understandable next to leasing and short-term rental.

What is live

carbnb.my walks through the offer, what is included, and how a subscription starts: choose a car, reserve it, and the concierge team delivers it. A comparison sets Carbnb beside leasing and rental so a visitor can see the difference without a sales call.

The Shiz partnered with Carbnb on this digital presence. There is no published testimonial from Carbnb on the-shiz.co, and we have not published a timeline or performance figures for the work.
