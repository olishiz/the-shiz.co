The brief

The Malaysia-Qatar Joint Business Council needed a public site that says, in plain language, what the council is for: fostering bilateral trade and investment between Malaysia and Qatar, and keeping members informed about opportunities in both countries.

What is live

The site introduces the council and its purpose, and gives members a place to find current information. The public contact point is Level 3, West Wing, Menara MATRADE, Kuala Lumpur, with email at admin@mqbc.com.my.

The Shiz designed and built that presence with Marisa at DMIA – MQBC. We have not published a timeline or performance figures for this project.
