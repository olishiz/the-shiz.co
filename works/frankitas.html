The brief

Frankitas sells handcrafted bags made from premium silk, woven with traditional techniques by artisans in Indonesia, Malaysia, Uzbekistan and Cambodia. The store had to carry that story and the collections — Titin, Puteri and Tasia — without looking like a generic catalogue.

What is live

frankitas.co opens on the craft and the collections, then the pieces themselves, priced in ringgit. The same site holds the longer story: natural fibres, plant-based dyes, and the workshops that finish each bag.

Oliver designed and built the storefront with Frankitas Turner. We have not published sales figures or a project timeline.
